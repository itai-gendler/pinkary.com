# Pinkary

Pinkary gives each User one profile page at /@username holding their links, a bio and badges, and turns it into a place to connect: others ask them questions, anonymously or not, which they answer publicly; they share posts, threads and polls, in channels and with hashtags; and everyone reads, likes, bookmarks, reposts and comments in the Recent, Following and Trending feeds. Users follow each other, are notified of what concerns them in the Product and by email, and earn a verified badge by sponsoring Pinkary on GitHub.

## Intent

"Pinkary is a landing page for all your links and a place to connect with like-minded individuals without the noise." It began as a way to share links in a more organized way and grew into a community with a feed, explore, questions and likes, built in the open as a community-driven project.
